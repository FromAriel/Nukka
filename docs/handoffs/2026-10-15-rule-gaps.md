# Handoff: Milestone 0 rule gaps fixed (2026-10-15)

Follows `2026-10-15-milestone-0.md`; its "What's next", "Notes" and tooling gotchas still apply.

## What was done

Fixed the three small rule gaps, pushed straight to `main` (one CI run):

1. **Covert Program revealed on use.** `CombatResult.assetsUsed` lists the installations and stacks that launched or fired. `applyCombatResult` calls `revealCovertPrograms` (how: `'used in combat'`) for the owner of any hidden one, before damage.
2. **Hardened partial hits persist.** `CombatResult.hardHits` carries each hit hardened installation's leftover; `applyCombatResult` writes it to `installation.hardHits` (0 if ruined).
3. **Tied neutral claims.** `CombatResult.tiedClaims` lists neutral territories cleared of defenders where attackers tied; they stay neutral with `match.neutral.militiaAfterTiedClaim` militia and a `NeutralClaimTied` event.

Tests in `packages/harness/src/combat.test.ts` (sim side) and `operations.test.ts` (applying, plus an end-to-end hidden-silo launch). `pnpm check`: 137 tests. `M0_Implementation_Notes.md` has two new timing notes.

## What we learned

- Equal formations attacking N-lakeshore from A- and B-heartland arrive level, so the autopilot sim produces a tie deterministically.
- At tension 100 a reveal's tension spike is clamped, so tests should check the event, not the tension change.
